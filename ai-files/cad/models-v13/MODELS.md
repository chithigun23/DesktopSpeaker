# models-v13: model provenance (2026-11-02)

All models are in `3d/` and were generated by `gen/build_parts_a.py` (board parts) or `gen/build_mech.py` (enclosure parts). Run them with `freecadcmd`. Each STEP has one coloured solid per feature, in mm. The colours are STYLED_ITEMs added by `gen/m13lib.py`, and kicad-cli renders show them.

**Download result.** No manufacturer STEP could be downloaded. TE, Coilcraft, Bourns, Microchip, Keystone and Würth returned 403. Molex and Alps did not respond. JST's download is form-gated. EasyEDA returned OBJ files only. The attempts are logged in `downloads/SOURCING-LOG.md`. Every new model below is therefore built from the maker drawing, or from secondary data where marked.

**Verification.**
- `gen/measure.py` measures each model in the footprint frame: the bounding box, the leads at board level against the SMD pads, and the pins at z -0.8 against the drill centres.
- The KiCad transform was checked against `kicad-cli pcb export step`. For J1, SW102, L2 and U1 the bounding boxes match exactly. File offsets are in the 3D frame with y up: offset y -0.427 moved U1 by 0.427 mm toward the footprint's bottom edge.
- Renders are in `renders/`.
