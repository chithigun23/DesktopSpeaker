# Critical power review — 2026-10-04

Read-only independent review of the active root and five power child sheets. I exported a fresh physical-pin XML netlist with installed KiCad 10.0.6 (`critical-power-current-netlist.xml`), inspected all six pages of `../DesktopSpeaker-preview.pdf`, and compared the local manufacturer datasheets with the active footprints and existing STEP bounds/alignment evidence. I did not edit schematics or libraries, run ERC, or build hardware. A netlist and a drawing cannot establish layout, transient, or production performance.

## Findings, in fix order

1. **High — confirmed unsafe first-power commissioning condition; already recorded as open.** `DesktopSpeaker-kicad/USB_PD.kicad_sch` U11/Q1/Q2/D6 and `Battery_Charger.kicad_sch` U4: the fresh netlist confirms Q1/Q2 pass `USB_VBUS` to U4 `VBUS` without an overvoltage interlock. The local [STUSB4500 datasheet](../datasheets/STUSB4500.pdf), §5.1, gives factory sink PDO2 **15 V/1.5 A** and PDO3 **20 V/1 A**. The [BQ25895 datasheet](../datasheets/BQ25895.pdf), §7.3, specifies **14 V maximum recommended VBUS**, though its non-switching absolute maximum is 22 V. The [SMBJ datasheet](../datasheets/Littelfuse_SMBJ.pdf) gives D6 SMBJ10A 10 V working standoff and 11.1–12.3 V breakdown: it is a pulse suppressor, not a sustained 15/20 V shunt. A PD-capable supply can therefore expose U4 and D6 to incompatible continuous voltage before firmware can intervene. **Action:** make programming *and readback* of a 5/9 V-only U11 NVM profile on a constrained 5 V source a mandatory, verified board commissioning gate before any PD-capable source is attached. If that production gate cannot be guaranteed, add hardware overvoltage blocking or select components that tolerate factory contracts. `plan.md` correctly leaves this open; schematic notes alone do not enforce it.

2. **Medium — source-aware charging policy lacks a runtime input; intentional unfinished integration.** `USB_PD.kicad_sch` U11/J4 and root `DesktopSpeaker.kicad_sch`: U11 SCL/SDA connect only to service header J4; its ALERT, ATTACH, POWER_OK2/3 and GPIO are unconnected. U4's I²C bus reaches the gauge-sheet pull-ups but not the currently unconnected MCU U3. The [STUSB4500 datasheet](../datasheets/STUSB4500.pdf), §§2.2.9–2.2.10 and 3.3, describes Type-C current/contract state and I²C negotiation data; the [BQ25895 datasheet](../datasheets/BQ25895.pdf), §8.2.6.2, makes IINLIM programmable and bounded by ILIM. Thus the planned source-dependent IINLIM/charge policy cannot yet run in the product. **Action:** when MCU integration is authorized, provide MCU access to PD contract/Type-C-current state and charger I²C with verified power-domain behavior, then gate charge enable until source and pack settings are read/set. The 220 Ω ILIM resistor is only a roughly 1.45–1.77 A hardware ceiling using the specified KILIM range; it does not distinguish a default-current USB source from a 1.5/3 A or PD source. The present default-off Q100 charge gate is a useful safeguard, not a completed policy.

3. **Medium — electronic-off battery draw needs a target-based design decision.** `Fuel_Gauge_Power.kicad_sch` U13 SEL1–3 and VDD are tied to `BAT_PACK`, so its three gauge channels remain enabled and the switch stays powered whenever SW101 is closed, including charger ship mode. The [TMUX1511 datasheet](../datasheets/TMUX1511.pdf), electrical characteristics, specifies 37 µA typical / 70 µA maximum supply current. That switch alone consumes about **0.89 mAh/day typical or 1.68 mAh/day maximum**; the handover's partial ~72–74 µA typical electronic-off sum corresponds to ~52–53 mAh/month before MCU, pack protection, leakage and other loads. Physical SW101 disconnect eliminates this board load, but electronic off has no defined battery-life target or measured budget. **Action:** set a storage-current target and choose whether the electronic-off mode must meet it; if so, revisit gauge-bus isolation using a lower-current solution that remains safe across BAT absence and up to a full 1S pack voltage. Do not simply power U13 from a disabled rail without rechecking powered-off signal ratings.

4. **Medium — regulator capacitor and switching margins remain unqualified; no demonstrated wrong connection.** `Bluetooth_Power.kicad_sch` U15/C150/C152–C154/L3 and `Logic_Audio_Power.kicad_sch` U14/C140–C142/L2: the correct nominal values and feedback topology are present in the current netlist. The [TPS63802 datasheet](../datasheets/TPS63802.pdf), §§8.3 and 10.2.2, requires effective input/output capacitance after DC bias and recommends two nominal 22 µF outputs above 3.6 V; the three fitted 22 µF outputs offer nominal headroom but no guaranteed corner proof is recorded. The [TPS61023 datasheet](../datasheets/TPS61023.pdf) likewise requires effective capacitance and switching/current checks at the intended load. **Action:** resolve effective capacitance, inductor saturation/transient and actual load corners before marking either supply electrically complete; verify board behavior once a PCB exists. The plan currently marks these reviews open, which is accurate.

## Geometry and mapping check

- `kicad-library/footprint/TPS63802DLAR.kicad_mod` pads 1–5 are the left row at 0.5 mm pitch; pads 10/9/8/7/6 form the right row. Their dimensions and centres match the [TPS63802 DLA0010A example board layout](../datasheets/TPS63802.pdf); pad 8 uses one electrical pad with two paste apertures. U15's symbol maps EN1, MODE2, AGND3, FB4, PG5, VOUT6, L2=7, GND8, L1=9, VIN10 to those pad numbers. The XML netlist confirms GND3/8, L3 between L1=9 and L2=7, VIN10 to SYS_RAW and VOUT6 to 3V8_BT. **No wrong U15 pad or net found.** STEP solid bounds and the saved alignment image support pin-1/terminal alignment. The user-supplied CAD download provenance is recorded but was not independently authenticated.
- `kicad-library/footprint/Coilcraft_XFL4015-471MEC.kicad_mod` has two 0.98 × 3.4 mm pads on 2.37 mm pitch, matching the [Coilcraft XFL4015 datasheet](../datasheets/Coilcraft_XFL4015.pdf) land pattern. Its STEP native vertical Y range −0.1 to 1.5 mm becomes board Z 0 to 1.6 mm after X +90° rotation and +0.1 mm Z offset; the existing alignment image is consistent. **No rotation or terminal mismatch found.** The datasheet marks a start/short lead to attach to the high-dV/dt node for lower EMI; L3 pad orientation should be chosen deliberately during later PCB layout, which is not authorized yet.
- Q1/Q2 common-source PMOS physical pads, Q100 2N7002 physical G/S/D, D5/D6 polarity, charger BAT/SYS/VBUS and gauge-switch S/D/SEL groups agree with the fresh netlist and the rendered pages. This verifies the schematic associations, not the future PCB routing or assemblies.

## Scope and status

The six-page preview is readable overall. IC labels are below bodies, passives/transistors to the right, child grounds point down, and the root power row is USB_PD → Battery_Charger → Fuel_Gauge_Power. Some long wires reflect the functional grouping; no ambiguous crossing was evident at the reviewed scale. Root MCU, Bluetooth/audio module, USB data, amplifiers, downstream rail loads, switches' actual MPN/rating, pack capacity/protection/NTC curve and firmware remain intentionally unfinished per `plan.md`; inherited ERC findings from those sections are not new defects. There is no claim of ERC clean status or complete product cost.
