# PCB layout rules: audio / RF / power (2026-10-06)

Basis: local datasheet layout sections (TAS5825M, BQ25792, TPS25730, PCM1862, TPA6132A2, BM83, TPS61023 as boost proxy) + web (links at end). Numbers marked (rule) are this report's choices, not datasheet limits; (DS) = datasheet. The TPS61088 datasheet is NOT in ai-files/datasheets (no file found): its layout rules below come from the TPS61023 boost note and general boost practice; fetch the real one before routing U25.

## 0. Datasheet findings that matter
- TAS5825M (DS): PVDD small caps (0.1/1 uF) "as close to PVDD pins as possible", farther than the example layout causes output ringing that can exceed abs-max (reliability, not only EMI); >22 uF low-ESL bulk on PVDD; BST caps (BST_x to OUT_x) adjacent to pins; keep contiguous ground from ground pins; thermal pad: pad no smaller than the package addendum, via drill <= 0.8 mm (DS says "8 mm", typo), no thermal relief on thermal vias, vias in columns radiating outward, aperture-array stencil 70-80 % coverage; keep away from board edge and other heat sources; higher layer count helps; example layout uses ~900 mm2 (11 cm2 total) copper area; route traces perpendicular to the device, orient passives narrow end toward device.
- BQ25792 (DS) priority list: (1) SYS caps, (2) PMID caps, (3) VBUS caps, each with a 0.1 uF 0402/0201 closer than the 10 uF caps; the cap-to-pin and cap-to-GND loop on TOP layer only, short, GND tied to IC ground by short trace or plane; SW1/SW2 inductor terminals as close as possible, minimum copper area on the switch nodes, minimum parasitic capacitance to other copper; REGN and bootstrap caps adjacent; thermal vias directly under power FETs (the IC), via size/count sized to current; route BATP/sense away from SW1/SW2.
- TPS25730D (DS): VBUS pours with >= 6 vias (8 mil hole/16 mil pad) top-to-bottom; VBUS_IN and PPHV >= 15 such vias; CC caps on the same side as the IC, close to CC1/CC2, no via between CC pin and CC cap (via after the cap); bottom drain pads of the example (back-to-back FET) must not be connected to anything; high speed data kept simple.
- PCM1862 (DS): one common ground plane (no AGND/DGND split), analog pins section kept away from digital pins, digital return currents kept away from AGND pin and inputs; decoupling on the same layer as the IC, NO vias between cap and pin; ground (pour/guard) between input traces for crosstalk.
- PCM2902C (DS): separate AGNDC/AGNDP/AGNDX/DGND/DGNDU pins must be within 0.1 V of each other: tie all to the one plane at the device; decoupling cap on VDDI (pin 27); keep crystal (XTI/XTO) short, with its ground island tied at AGNDX.
- TPA6132A2 (DS): exposed pad to ground only (never to a supply), same ground as PGND pin 10, must be soldered; charge-pump flying cap and rails local.
- BM83 (DS ch. 7): antenna keep-out: no copper, no components on ANY layer under the antenna; no ground plane surrounding the antenna; keep all external metal >= 15 mm from the PCB trace antenna (best range); continuous ground plane at least module size (16 x 19 mm) under the module body with distributed via stitching, no trace routing under the module on the top layer; reference radiation data used a 45 x 45 mm carrier; module FCC/ISED needs the ground plane; small cut-out under the RF test pad allowed. Antenna should be at the board edge, free to air.
- STM32G071: decoupling 100 nF per VDD pin + 4.7 uF bulk, VDDA 100 nF + 1 uF, close to pins; crystal (if used) load caps and short traces, ground guard (DS Fig. 13 power decoupling).
- MAX17048: keep sense resistor-free layout simple; 0.1 uF at CELL/VDD; keep away from switch nodes (only a hint in the datasheet).

## 1. Stackup (JLCPCB 4-layer, 1.6 mm)
JLC capability page (fetched 2026-10-06): 4-layer min track/space 0.10/0.10 mm (1 oz outer), 0.16/0.16 mm (2 oz outer); via drill 0.15-6.3 mm, via diameter >= 0.25 mm standard (0.2 mm only on <=1 mm boards); annular ring >= 0.20 mm PTH (0.254 mm+ for 2 oz); via hole-to-hole 0.2 mm, pad hole-to-hole 0.45 mm; outer copper 1 or 2 oz, inner 0.5 (default) or 1 oz; thickness 0.8/1.0/1.2/1.6/2.0; filled-and-capped via-in-pad 0.15-0.55 mm drill; mask bridge 0.10 mm.
Recommended: **1.6 mm, JLC04161H-7628 family**, order with outer 1 oz (2 oz outer only for the PVDD/VBUS/battery zone if tracks cannot be wide enough; 2 oz outer forces 0.16/0.16 rules on the whole board and 6.5 mil fine pitch fails the J1/U11 pads), inner 1 oz (not 0.5 oz: the inner planes carry PVDD/SYS currents).
| Layer | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| L1 F.Cu | components, short signals, local power, switch nodes | all high-frequency loops stay on L1 |
| L2 In1 | **solid GND, no splits**, no routing except unavoidable 1-2 jumpers | reference plane for L1; prepreg 0.21 mm (7628) to L1 gives tight coupling |
| L3 In2 | power pours (PVDD_AMP, SYS_RAW, 5V, 3V3 islands) + few slow signals | adjacent to GND L2 only via core (about 1.06 mm in the JLC 1.6 mm stack, loosely coupled: do not rely on L2/L3 capacitance) |
| L4 B.Cu | GND pour + slow signals/test points, no components | stitch to L2 |
Alternative if audio performance demands it: L1 sig, L2 GND, L3 GND, L4 sig and power as wide pours on L1/L4. Not necessary: JLC thickness table verifies 1.6 mm; confirm the exact JLC04161H layer data in the JLC impedance tool (the fetched table shows 1.1 mm total, i.e. a different variant than 1.6 mm; pick the 1.6 mm variant explicitly).
Controlled impedance: only USB D+/D- (90 ohm diff, +-15 %). Over L2 with 0.21 mm prepreg, Er 4.4: estimated start **w 0.20 mm / gap 0.15 mm** (rule, to be confirmed in the JLC impedance calculator with the chosen stackup; order with "impedance control" note or accept that USB 2.0 full-speed (PCM2902C is 12 Mbit/s) and the PD USB path tolerate +-20 %). USB routes < 30 mm, so impedance control is low-risk: still route as a pair, length-matched within 0.5 mm, ground-referenced on L2, no splits underneath. No 50 ohm line unless the BM83 RF test pad is used (no antenna feed on the board; module has its own antenna).

## 2. Zone rules and minimum separation
Frame = placement report frame. Today: BM83 left edge, charger/PD right, amps front centre, audio centre/rear, boost front right.
| Pair | Min distance (rule) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| BM83 antenna end to any copper/part (any layer) | 15 mm external metal (DS); 3 mm none under antenna (any layer); keep-out strip 0.4x the module width | DS; present 3 mm placement margin is a bare minimum |
| BM83 body to switching nodes (SW1/SW2, L200, L201-206, OUT nodes) | >= 25 mm | 2.4 GHz desense from class-D (400-1 MHz harmonics) and boost |
| BM83 to analogue audio inputs (PCM1862, J2/J3, mux) | >= 15 mm; no analogue trace under or beside the module | RF pickup at the 2 Vpp lines; add RF filter (ferrite+100 pF) at jacks |
| Class-D/boost power (L200, L201-206, U6/U7 outputs) to analogue audio inputs | >= 20 mm (10 mm absolute minimum with ground fence) | magnetic coupling to high-impedance ADC input nodes |
| Switch-node copper to anything | > 2 mm clearance, no routes under the node on L1; L2 GND solid below | capacitive coupling |
| Digital clocks (I2S BCLK/MCLK) to analogue | >= 5 mm and never parallel; series 22-33 ohm on source | |
| USB data to class-D/boost | >= 10 mm; stitch ground both sides | |
Ground handling (one solid plane, as PCM1862 DS and TI mixed-signal guidance): no plane splits; instead partition by component placement so digital/class-D returns never travel under analogue inputs. PGND vs AGND: all ground pins land on the one plane, but connect each IC's AGND pin at the IC (star at the thermal pad for TAS5825M x2), and put PVDD bulk/output-filter caps' returns in the amp's own neighbourhood. Keep the headphone amp (TPA6132A2, capless) and the ADC input return paths along their own corridor with the left jack/ADC; no PGND currents cross it. Guard: GND via fence (0.5 mm via, 2 mm pitch) between (a) the amp block and codecs, (b) BM83 and everything else except its own supply, (c) both sides of USB pair. Via stitching elsewhere: 4-5 mm grid on L1/L4 GND pours, every <= 2.5 mm along the board edge near the BM83 (lambda/20 at 2.45 GHz is about 3 mm in FR4: stitch <= 3 mm near RF). Ground stitching vias at each decoupling cap return.
Audio signal routing: 2 Vpp input signals as differential-aware pairs (route the two conductors together, or signal + its own AGND return track side-by-side, 0.3 mm apart), 0.2 mm min width (0.25 rule), 3x width spacing (>= 0.6 mm) to any other net, on L1 over unbroken L2 GND; no vias where avoidable; ground pour between input traces (PCM1862 DS); high-impedance nodes short; place jack-side series R and filter C at the jack; the capless TPA6132A2 outputs routed as a pair away from the amp block. Keep all audio on L1 (no L3/L4 detours).
Class-D: TAS5825M output path OUT_x to inductor to cap to speaker connector in tight loops (inductor adjacent to OUT pins, filter cap grounded to PGND of that amp at the amp's ground pins, 0.1 uF/1 uF bulk per PVDD pin), BTL pair outputs routed as parallel traces 0.3 mm apart (rule) on L1, <= 40 mm. Present placement has the 2.4 mm gap between inductor columns and the inductors 2 mm from the output caps: tighten later; loop area of L-to-C-to-ground should be < 100 mm2 (rule) (EMI is "size of the loop" per TI class-D layout notes).

## 3. Single board vs two boards
**Recommendation: keep a single 4-layer board (grow to about 120 x 96 mm only if needed), with the zone rules above; carry the two-board split as a documented fallback.** Reasons: all noise-sensitive audio is analogue input (2 Vpp, line-level, 20-bit-class ADC) and headphone out, not microvolt sensors; the datasheets (PCM1862) explicitly allow a single plane; the shield-by-distance requirement is 20-25 mm, achievable in 114 x 92 mm after rotating the audio block to the opposite edge from the amp block; a stacked board adds board-to-board connector crosstalk and ground-current paths, which usually degrades analogue performance unless the connector grounds are many. The current placement worry (U6/U7 and the inductor block adjacent to U24, U2 and the headphone amp) is a placement problem solved by moving the audio block, not by splitting.
Triggers for the split: (i) after layout, class-D/boost less than 20 mm from the ADC inputs cannot be avoided; (ii) first-prototype noise floor measurement (idle output, ADC SNR) fails by > 6 dB; (iii) BM83 range measurement fails.
If split: **Board P (power/amp):** USB_PD, Battery_Charger, Fuel_Gauge_Power, Amplifiers (TPS61088, 2x TAS5825M, speaker connectors), MCU (STM32G071 controls both; its USB/SWD there). **Board A (audio/RF, upper board, nearest the cabinet edge for antenna):** BM83, PCM1862, PCM2902C, TS5A23157 muxes, TPA6132A2, jacks, USB audio. Interface: 3 I2S (BCLK, LRCK, DATA each, plus MCLK if used = 9-12 lines), 2 I2C (SDA, SCL x2 = 4, with a 3.3 V level and pull-ups on P side), enables/faults (about 6: AMP_EN, AMP_FAULT_N, BT_RESET, PWR_EN etc.), rails 3V3/5V (2-3 pins each, 100 mA-1 A), GND interleaved: 1 GND pin after every 2 signals, minimum 8 GND total. About 40 pins: use a 2 x 20 0.8 mm board-to-board receptacle (stack height 5-8 mm), or 2 x 20 2.54 mm pin header/socket for prototypes. Put the analogue-audio-only rails (3V_AO, 3V3_AUDIO LDOs) on Board A, fed from a single 5 V pin with a ferrite. The root hierarchy already maps: Board A = BM83 + audio sheets; Board P = the rest.
Netlist consequence: sheet-level ports I2S_*, SDA/SCL, ENx, 5V_LOGIC, 3V3 and GND become connector nets (J_B2B); no schematic change is needed now, only a connector footprint at split time.

## 4. Trace widths (2x current margin, IPC-2221/2152 style)
Method: IPC-2221 external formula A = (I/(0.048*dT^0.44))^(1/0.725), dT = 10 K, applied at **2 x I** (the over-specified margin the user requested; IPC-2152 with an adjacent plane would allow narrower, so this is conservative). Copper: 1 oz = 35 um, 2 oz = 70 um. Inner layers carry about 2x the width of outer for the same rise (IPC-2221 internal derating) - use pours only.

| VBUS_PD, VBUS (20 V, up to 3 A; 5 A cable only if enabled: designed 3 A) | 3 A | **3.5 mm** (pour) | 1.8 mm | **0.4 mm** (22 V TVS rail) | pour + >= 6 vias 0.4/0.2 mm (TPS25730 DS) per layer change |

Vias (JLC): standard signal 0.3 mm drill / 0.6 mm pad (comfortably above 0.20 mm ring and 0.25 mm via min); power 0.4 mm / 0.8 mm (capacity about 1 A each: use I/1 A x 2 margin: 3 A needs 6, 6 A needs 12, 9 A needs 18); thermal vias 0.3 mm drill 1.0 mm pitch, no thermal relief, no tenting needed on the underside (JLC filled-and-capped 0.15-0.55 mm optional).
Amp vias (TAS5825M x2): thermal pad needs the 6 x 4 (or package addendum) pad array of 0.3 mm vias at 1.0-1.2 mm pitch (>= 20 vias), arranged in columns radiating outward (DS), connecting to L2 GND and L4 pour (>= 900 mm2 on L4 under/around each amp, no components there). Boost U25: >= 9 thermal vias in its pad. BQ25792: >= 12 thermal vias, SW1/SW2 pins 4+ vias each only to the L1 inductor pads (no inner layer connection of SW nodes). TPS25730: use DS counts above (6 VBUS, 15 VBUS_IN/PPHV).

## 5. Teardrops (KiCad 10, Board Setup > Design Rules > Teardrops, also Edit > Teardrops)
- Enable for all three groups: round pads, rectangular pads (and SMD pads: "round pads" group covers circular pads; rectangular/oval pads are in the rect group), track-to-track (T-junctions between different width tracks), and vias (vias set under the round pad group in KiCad 10: set all four "target" checkboxes).
- Settings (rule, conservative for JLC): Best length ratio 0.5 (best), max length 1.0 mm, best width ratio 1.0, max width 2.0 mm (power pours tolerate 3.0 mm), curved edges on, 5 curve points; "Maximum track width" = 0 (all), min track width 0.2 mm, **"Prefer zone connection" off** for power pads (so teardrops are not absorbed into a pour), on for GND.
- Apply to every track-pad and track-via connection (Edit > Add Teardrops with "Apply to all"; check "Do not create teardrops for track ends sharing pads"). Re-run after any routing change (teardrops are zones; the DRC will report them as filled zone changes).
- Constraints: min track length 0.5 mm so the teardrop has room; for 0402 hand-solder pads (0.5 x 0.55 mm) a track narrower than the pad (0.25 mm) gets a short teardrop (length cap about 0.5 mm: set max length 0.6 mm for these via per-footprint override or a custom rule); keep the teardrop away from adjacent pad within clearance (KiCad suppresses violations, but check DRC); for the 0.4 mm-pitch QFN pads (TPS25730 and similar) teardrops are limited and automatically skipped: that is acceptable, verify DRC.
- Teardrops are mandatory on all power vias, USB/audio vias, and at the entry to the BM83 castellated pads and connector pads (reduces annular-ring breakout and mechanical stress).

## 8. Decoupling practice (added for placement v4, 2026-10-06)
Lookups (4 searches/fetches; fetched summaries only, numbers marked (rule) are ours): nextpcb placement rules https://www.nextpcb.com/blog/decoupling-capacitor-placement-pcb ; TI SLVAE04 (cap ground to the IC ground pin by the shortest way) https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slvae04/slvae04.pdf ; TI SLUA457 / SLOA069 decoupling notes https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slua457/slua457.pdf , https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sloa069/sloa069.pdf ; TI E2E MSP430 decoupling thread https://e2e.ti.com/support/microcontrollers/msp-low-power-microcontrollers-group/msp430/f/msp-low-power-microcontroller-forum/537283/decoupling-capacitance . Plus the local datasheet layout sections quoted in section 0.
1. **Order**: smallest value (0.1 uF / 1 nF, 0402) nearest the pin, then the 1-10 uF, then bulk (22 uF+); bulk may sit 4-6 mm away, the small cap may not.
2. **Loop**: pin -> cap pad -> GND via -> plane is the loop to minimise; every mm of trace adds about 1 nH, a 0.3 mm via 0.3-0.5 nH. Cap pad centre <= 2-2.5 mm from the IC pin it serves (critical: PVDD, SYS, PMID, VBUS, AVDD, VCC), <= 3 mm others (rule).
3. **Geometry**: cap long axis along the pin-to-pad direction, the power pad towards the pin, the GND pad away from it, so a GND via fits beside the GND pad. Short, wide connections (pad-width track, or a pour on L1), no via between cap and supply pin.
4. **Vias**: GND via adjacent to the cap GND pad on the same side (<= 1 mm); no via-in-pad because 0402 is hand-soldered (solder wicks into the via). Via-in-pad is the lowest-inductance option and is only acceptable with filled/capped vias in assembly.
5. **Power entry (cap first, then pin)**: power should reach the pin through the cap pad (cap pad on the supply track, track continues to the pin) so the cap sees the current first; the "via first" variant (feed via, then cap, then pin) is used when the supply comes from an inner plane: place the supply via beside the cap's power pad, never beyond the pin. One cap per supply pin pair; do not share one cap between pins on opposite sides.
6. **Per IC (DS)**: TAS5825M 0.1/1 uF on every PVDD pin pair as close as the example (ringing can exceed abs max), BST caps at the pins, >= 22 uF low-ESL <= 6 mm; BQ25792 SYS, PMID, VBUS each 0.1 uF closer than 10 uF, loop on L1 only, REGN/BTST at the pin; TPS61088 (datasheet missing; rule from section 6) VIN/VOUT caps <= 2 mm, FB divider at FB away from SW; PCM1862 supply 0.1 uF on the same layer with no via between cap and pin; STM32G071 100 nF per VDD + 4.7 uF, VDDA 100 nF + 1 uF; BM83 10 uF + 0.1 uF + 1 nF at the supply pads; TPS25730D CC caps close to CC1/CC2 with the via after the cap, LDO caps <= 2 mm.
7. **Limit seen in v4**: the first ring of a 5 mm QFN holds only about 3 caps per side once each part carries a 0.8 mm reference label and a 0.5 mm courtyard gap; second-ring caps land at 3.5-5 mm. Fix options: labels of first-ring caps on a second (fab) silkscreen layer, or 0201 caps for the HF values.
