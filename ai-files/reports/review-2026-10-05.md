# Current schematic review — 2026-10-05

Read-only review: active KiCad project and isolated TPS25730D/BQ25792 drafts. No circuit fixes or PCB layout were made. Active hardware remains 5/9 V only; 20 V operation is a replacement design in progress.

## Findings, in priority order

1. **Active gauge interface has a low-battery voltage-domain gap.** U13/U16/U17 TS5A3167 supplies follow BAT_PACK, but their COM-side pullups remain at 3V_AO when USB is present. Below 3 V battery voltage this exceeds the switches' recommended analog voltage range; U5 MAX17048 is outside its operating range below 2.5 V. The specified VCC=0 isolation does not establish safe behavior at intermediate battery voltages. Change isolation supply/control or use appropriate translation with a qualified default-off state. This is a confirmed operating-range mismatch, not a measured failure. See [independent electrical review](electrical-review-2026-10-05.md).
2. **Replacement charger purchasing metadata is wrong for R102.** Displayed 4.7 kΩ correctly selects 1S/750 kHz, but hidden MPN RC0603FR-07220RL / C107696 specifies 220 Ω. Correct purchasing fields before integration. C110/C113/C114, R108–R112 and Q101/Q102 also have no selected purchasing fields in the draft export; reconcile them rather than treating the active BOM as a replacement BOM.
3. **USB current permission/startup is unfinished.** ILIM_HIZ sampled low at POR caches a 100 mA clamp; release does not document resampling. Clearing EN_EXTILIM removes the proposed external current ceiling. Complete source classification, configuration/readback, whole-board current budgeting and watchdog/reset recovery before releasing HIZ. Existing active BQ25895 startup limitations remain until replaced. See [ILIM review](bq25792-ilim-startup-review.md).
4. **Electronic ship mode needs explicit firmware configuration.** Q103 and mechanical SW101 are in series as intended in the replacement draft. TI REG14 SFET_PRESENT defaults to zero and locks ship-control fields until set to one. Add this to verified startup/reset policy. This is a missing implementation requirement, not an incorrect FET connection.
5. **Replacement drafts fail the requested readability standard.** The TPS25730D helper explicitly hides both pin names and pin numbers; its rendered page has detached labeled circuitry and overlapping notes. The BQ draft has excessive local labels, GND labels instead of ground symbols, crowded pin text and switch/connector overlap. Redraw both with visible functional pins, direct wiring and compact grouped placement, then compare every physical pin net before integration.
6. **Protection and source/load corners remain qualification gates.** TI-recommended TVS2200 has a specified maximum clamp above TPS25730's absolute maximum; measured pin transients and protection coordination are unresolved. Bound U19 weak-5-V startup/dropout with actual MCU load, low-pack mux switchover/BOR, converter effective capacitance, L1/audio/charge thermal limits and the final pack NTC/current rating. These are not claimed measured defects.
