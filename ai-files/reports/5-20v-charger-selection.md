# 5–20 V, 1S charger architecture selection

Research snapshot: 2026-10-05. Selection is a recommendation for main integration review; no active schematic, library, or BOM files were edited.

**Coordinator correction (2026-10-05):** The first report incorrectly read BQ25672's minimum input as 6 V. Its recommended-operating-conditions table specifies 3.6–24 V (also confirmed by VBUS_OP), so BQ25672 supports both 5 V startup and 20 V PD. The electrical comparison below retains it as a valid alternative. The coordinator selected BQ25792RQMR for capture because it costs less in the LCSC snapshot, has the same quoted low-power current, and its buck-boost path provides better conversion headroom from weak 5 V sources without a separate pre-buck stage. The prior BQ25672 lead recommendation is superseded.

## Recommendation

Advance **TI BQ25792RQMR (LCSC C2862876)** as the selected U4 architecture. It supports 1–4 cells, specifies 3.6–24 V recommended VBUS, and allows 5 V startup and 20 V PD. Its buck-boost stage supports direct power-path operation across the input/cell range. Its 30 V absolute maximum is a damage boundary, not an operating target; at 20 V allow transient margin and keep VBUS within the recommended range through coordinated protection.

Keep **BQ25672RQMR (C3682029)** as the valid higher-cost alternative. It supports 1–4 cells, 3.6–24 V recommended input and 3 A charging. BQ25798 is a further alternate mainly if MPPT or fast backup becomes a requirement.

All three share a 29-pin 4×4 mm package class, integrated switching and battery-path FETs, I2C/ADC, thermistor monitoring, programmable current limits, and D+/D− adapter detection. BQ25672 is marketed as a buck charger with boost operation for OTG; BQ25792 is the buck-boost charging variant. BQ25672 has a 1S PROG-pin option and 1 A POR charge-current setting, so its 3 A maximum is already ample for a conservative 10 Ah pack target. Its MPPT and second-input mux can remain unused in the single-input USB design. BQ25792's advantages here are its 5 A ceiling and lower sampled LCSC price, not a required charging feature.

All support adapter-powered SYS with a missing battery when charging is disabled and minimum system voltage configured. SYS is an NVDC battery-like rail, not fixed 5 V. Detect the battery/NTC at startup and keep charging disabled until they are valid; the TI datasheets warn that charge-enabled no-battery operation can cause BAT/SYS voltage excursions. Confirm the exact 1S boot sequence before capture.

Absolute maximum ratings are damage boundaries, not operating targets. All three candidate operating ranges include 20 V, but that does not prove hot-plug or surge survival. TI recommends an input capacitor when hot-plugging adapters above 15 V; retain that provision and coordinate it with the USB-C protection path.

## Proposed first-pass configuration

- Keep the existing USB-PD controller as the contract authority. Request/accept only 5 V during default startup; expose 9/15/20 V operation only after the source contract and input protection state are valid. The charger does not negotiate USB-PD contracts itself.
- Configure a **1S protected Li-ion pack**, nominally about 10 Ah and 10 kΩ NTC. Match TS thresholds and any JEITA settings to the actual pack thermistor curve, not merely its nominal resistance.
- Start with **1.0 A fast-charge current** (0.1 C for a 10 Ah pack) as a conservative provisional point, subject to the pack maker's permitted rate and thermal testing. Lower to 0.5 A if needed. Do not treat either device's 3 A/5 A capability as the pack's rating. Program a chemistry-appropriate cell voltage only after pack selection; leave charge disabled until pack/NTC/source checks pass.
- Use the charger input-current limit for source budgeting. BQ25672 and BQ2579x POR IINDPM is 3 A; D+/D− detection may set up to 3.25 A for DCP. Do not rely on reset/detection values for weak or unknown legacy sources. Set a hardware ILIM_HIZ clamp to limit POR and autonomous detection, then raise it only after source classification or a valid PD contract. `VILIM_HIZ = 1 V + 0.8 Ω × IINDPM`; 500 mA corresponds to 1.4 V. Verify divider tolerance, ADC quantization and startup sequencing. A 500 mA clamp does **not** certify USB default-current compliance; unknown USB-host sources need an independent compliant policy. Never infer 2 A entitlement from the reset value or adapter label.
- The 5 V / 2 A legacy supply remains supported by operating from 5 V and limiting charger input current and charge current to leave headroom for the speaker load. Start with no more than 1.0 A charging on this input; firmware should reduce charging first and then system/audio demand when input-current or VINDPM regulation occurs. At 1 A charge and 5 V, the charger already needs roughly 0.9–1.0 A input before SYS loads (assuming 85–90% conversion efficiency); at 20 V the same battery charge power is about 0.25 A before SYS loads. These are planning estimates, not thermal or runtime guarantees.
- Preserve direct external-power operation with battery absent through the charger SYS output. With battery present, allow battery supplement only within verified pack, BATFET, connector, and protection-current limits. BQ25798 recommended continuous battery discharge is 6 A RMS and 10 A peak up to 1 s; apply those values only to that IC's operating envelope, not as a substitute for pack/protection ratings. The speaker/audio peak budget remains open.
- The initial target charge current is far below either device's maximum; the shared power stage can still dissipate meaningful heat when supplying SYS plus charge. Use the datasheet thermal regulation and pack NTC, provide copper/thermal vias per TI layout guidance, and verify temperature at worst-case 5 V, 20 V, depleted-cell and battery-absent loads. The quoted 44.2 °C/W package thermal metric is board-condition dependent, not an in-product thermal prediction.

## Inductor and passives

Use the TI application topology and do not inherit BQ25895's 2.2 µH / 6 A inductor blindly. Both BQ25672 and BQ25792 example at 1.5 MHz with **1.0 µH**; the 750 kHz option uses **2.2 µH**. TI's 1 µH example is Sunlord SPM6530T-1R0M120. Select saturation current above the greater of input or charge current plus half worst-case ripple, with low DCR and suitable RMS/thermal rating. The 1 µH choice fits the proposed 1 A charge target, but verify audio load peaks and PCB copper.

For BQ25672, the TI single-input example uses VBUS 2×10 µF plus 0.1 µF, PMID 3×10 µF plus 0.1 µF, SYS 3×10 µF plus 0.1 µF, BAT 2×10 µF, plus bootstrap and REGN/TS parts. Meet its **effective** minimums after DC-bias/tolerance: VBUS 2 µF, PMID 4 µF, SYS 6 µF, BAT 3 µF. BQ25792's example uses five 10 µF SYS capacitors and otherwise similar banks. Rate VBUS/PMID caps 35–50 V after transient analysis; do not use 25 V parts on a 20 V input without derating evidence. BQ25798 backup mode raises PMID effective capacitance to 70 µF. Recalculate inrush/hot-plug current with full effective capacitance.

## Interface / redesign consequences

- The existing U4 BQ25895 buck charger cannot accept 20 V, and the current USB input TPS26600 path is configured to reject high-voltage contracts. Moving to either BQ25672 or BQ2579x is an architecture change: upstream pass FET/eFuse/TVS/OVP, PD policy, capacitor ratings, VBUS sensing and SYS assumptions need coordinated review. The pending LTC4368/NFET path must be checked for 20 V conduction and a separate appropriate overvoltage cutoff.
- Keep downstream rails designed for charger SYS's 1S/NVDC voltage range. The current SYS_RAW-based converters already expect a low-voltage battery-like input, but confirm their minimum startup voltage under depleted-cell and battery-absent conditions and that their input maxima tolerate SYS transitions.
- Preserve separate physical pack disconnect and charger electronic ship/off. SDRV is a driver for an **external** ship FET; it does not switch that path itself. The datasheet allows omitting the external FET, but internal BATFET ship behavior is revision/device-specific: BQ25672 describes BATFET off in ship mode; BQ25792 Rev. C says it reconnects the internal BATFET on exit; the current BQ25798 Rev. C deleted a prior statement that its BATFET turns off in ship mode. For a guaranteed system disconnect with button/USB wake, include the SDRV-controlled external series ship FET or verify the selected device's internal BATFET behavior and residual SYS leakage from the exact current datasheet. SW101 remains the independent mechanical pack disconnect. Budget charger, gauge, protection and board leakage. Shutdown has lower charger current but adapter-only wake.
- I2C address, pin assignment, package, pull-up rail and shared gauge bus need pin-level review before replacing U4. All candidates are 29-pin 4×4 mm QFN and are not drop-ins for the current charger symbol/footprint. Keep charge disabled by default and verify QON wake and ship-FET wiring against the selected revision.

## Capture brief — Battery_Charger replacement (2026-10-05)

This is the bounded implementation brief for the library handoff. **Do not capture until the coordinator reviews the unregistered BQ25792 library candidate.** Keep current refs U4/J5/SW100/SW101/Q100 and existing interfaces `VBUS_PD`, `SYS_RAW`, `BAT_PACK`, `CHG_*`, and `CHG_VIO_3V0`/3VAO. Do not add MCU/audio connections or change the root hierarchy in this capture.

### Configuration and policy

- Fit BQ25792RQMR (C2862876), configured as **1S** by `PROG = 4.7 kΩ` for **750 kHz**. Keep the existing 2.2 µH L1 as a candidate only; its 6 A rated current and 9.5 A saturation rating need ripple, DCR, thermal, SYS peak-load, and layout checks against the new buck-boost stage before signoff.
- Target **1 A charging** for the nominal 10 Ah protected pack; do not let the 2 A POR charge setting take effect. CE (`CHG_ENABLE`) remains charge-only and default-disabled. Set charge voltage/termination and TS thresholds only after the actual cell and 10 kΩ NTC curve are known. Battery absent: charging off; confirm SYS-only regulation and SYS_MIN startup experimentally.
- Preserve the independent mechanical disconnect SW101. BQ25792 SDRV requires an **external series N-channel ship FET** between protected pack/BATP and the internal BAT path. BATP is on the pack side; the gauge and charger BAT/internal BATFET/SYS side must follow the exact TI Figure 9-14 connectivity. QON remains SW100 to ground; POR wake-low duration is typically 1 s, or 15 ms when WKUP_DLY is programmed, and approximately 10 s low invokes system power reset. Retain the button path and label hold-time behavior for validation.
- Keep a default hardware clamp on `ILIM_HIZ` so the converter cannot start from POR. Add a distinct default-off hierarchy control, preferably `CHG_SYS_ENABLE`, that releases that clamp only after the raw-powered controller has identified the source and programmed I2C limits. Do not reuse `CHG_ENABLE` for SYS startup. Pull the clamp node low below 0.75 V to stop switching while REGN remains on; drive above 1 V only when the policy permits. The pin formula is `VILIM_HIZ = 1 V + 0.8 Ω × I`; use a USB-only regulated rail for the clamp/reference so the divider does not drain the battery.
- Datasheet power-up sequence confirms I2C becomes available about 20 ms after valid input; REGN starts about 150 ms later. Poor-source qualification runs after REGN, reads ILIM_HIZ, and is followed by D+/D− detection before converter startup. Thus hold ILIM_HIZ low during qualification: pin-low HIZ is distinct from `EN_HIZ`, and the documented sequence still runs DPDM. The pin-low state disables switching, not REGN/DPDM. Bench-check DPDM completion/status and VBUS current with converter held off before enabling SYS.
- PD-current interface is controller-specific. With the currently captured STUSB4500, do not assume TPS25730 register semantics: no undocumented STUSB4500 claim for active-contract current at 0x34/0x35 is made here, and the captured path does not expose USB-C Rp status to the MCU. If the separate TPS25730 controller is adopted, its accepted-contract-current registers 0x34/0x35 can inform the PD limit. For non-PD legacy sources use charger BC1.2/DPDM result and a conservative cap; do not infer 2 A from a 5 V / 2 A adapter label. The exact minimum fallback policy remains unresolved (including USB default-current compliance); limit charging to 0 A until classification, then allocate source current between SYS/audio and charging. Digital IINDPM is the active lower source ceiling after enabling the converter. Configure it before release of `CHG_SYS_ENABLE`.
- The MCU must boot from USB raw auxiliary power independently of SYS. U19 TPS7B8450 currently outputs the captured net `PD_VDD_5V`; repurposing it as a raw-MCU auxiliary rail (proposed `USB_AUX5V` role/name), routing it to MCU/3VAO, or adding a USB-only regulator are architecture options, not existing charger-sheet wiring. Keep that rail isolated from BAT_PACK and do not draw MCU standby current through a battery divider. Existing U12 3VAO fed by SYS_RAW cannot by itself perform source classification with an absent battery and ILIM_HIZ held low. No MCU connections are in scope here.
- The BQ25792 data table gives ship-current 11 µA typ / 16 µA max at VBAT=8 V, I2C on and ADC off; this 8 V test point is not the 1S operating condition. Retain **23 µA as a conservative architecture standby allocation** pending system-level leakage measurement; it is not the BQ25792 datasheet maximum and is not a guaranteed 1S value. Shutdown is lower current but adapter-only wake; ship mode plus QON/adapter wake is the intended electronic-off behavior.

### Capture deltas and review points

- Add the second 47 nF bootstrap capacitor for the second switching leg (reuse existing C103 for one leg only after confirming pad mapping). Add an input ceramic bank on VBUS sized for effective capacitance and DC bias at 20 V, rated 35–50 V; current C100 is 2.2 µF/50 V and alone is not the desired bank. In the TI power-path topology PMID follows the high input path, so assess the existing 25 V PMID capacitors for DC-bias derating and transients; do not presume PMID is a low-voltage node. Keep existing SYS/BAT/REGN values only where node ratings and TI effective-minimum guidance remain valid.
- Existing `BAT_PACK` port semantics and J5 thermistor/ground are retained. Verify physical external-SFET series location and orientation before wiring: protected pack → SW101 → BAT_PACK/BATP → ship FET → charger BAT/internal BATFET → SYS. Confirm no parallel gauge or return path bypasses the ship FET. Add a suitable SDRV capacitor per TI; use small-gate-charge low-RDS(on) device to limit charge-pump wake time, then check peak pack current and dissipation.
- Preserve Q100's standard transistor mapping and /CE default-off circuit. Retain charger I2C, INT, TS, REGN, `VBUS_PD`, `SYS_RAW`, `BAT_PACK` and 3VAO interface names; no direct external-power-to-SYS bypass is added. Keep OTG disabled. The charger SYS output supports battery-absent operation through its power path, subject to startup/thermal validation.
- BQ25792 typical input operating range is 3.6–24 V, with 30 V **absolute maximum**; 5 V and 20 V are both within recommended operation. Protection must constrain hot-plug/ringing below the operating ceiling. This does not waive redesign of the existing 5/9 V-only TPS26600 path, VBUS sense, and USB-PD contract policy.
- Pre-capture reviews: verify the library footprint pin map; recheck the selected L1 ripple and thermal margin with the load budget; size/qualify ILIM_HIZ pull-up and default-low clamp tolerance; check ship-FET SDRV wake-time and exact pin orientation; confirm SYS_MIN behavior and 1S battery-absent startup; inspect 20 V surge/OVP coordination and capacitor derating. Do not claim 100 mA legacy-source compliance from ILIM_HIZ alone.
