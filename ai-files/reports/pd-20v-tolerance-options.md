# Accidental 15/20 V USB PD tolerance — architecture recommendation

Date: 2026-10-04. Scope: 5/9 V operation; survive a sustained accidental factory 15 V or 20 V contract (including about +5%, 21 V), without using that higher voltage for charging. This is a read-only architecture and sourcing review of the active project as found before implementation. Board surge/ESD qualification remains a separate test.

## Recommendation: block high contracts ahead of BQ25895

Keep STUSB4500, Q1/Q2 and BQ25895. Insert **TI TPS26600PWPR** (HTSSOP-16-EP; LCSC **C544399**) in series between the common-source PMOS pair's existing output and the `VBUS_PD` child-sheet port. This makes `VBUS_PD` the protected rail delivered to the charger. The TPS26600 is rated for 4.2–60 V operation, has integrated reverse-blocking FETs, and provides adjustable *cut-off* overvoltage protection. The similarly named **TPS26602 has a 38 V clamp, not the needed 11 V cut-off**. TI specifies 150 mΩ typical, 250 mΩ maximum total on resistance; at 1.8 A that is 0.27 V typical, 0.45 V maximum, and 0.49/0.81 W respectively. The connector/source voltage and BQ's 3.9 V minimum must therefore still be budgeted at the weak 5 V corner. A 5 V/2 A adapter remains supported within the existing approximately 1.45–1.77 A BQ hardware ILIM ceiling and source-aware firmware limits. TPS26600's 300 µA typical/390 µA maximum operating current is drawn from external VBUS, not the battery when USB is absent. [TI TPS2660 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf), §§7.5 and 9.3.

Proposed TPS26600 configuration for the schematic: OVP divider from eFuse IN to OVP to RTN of **100 kΩ / 12.1 kΩ, 1%**. With TI's 1.17–1.225 V rising comparator threshold and resistor corners, the trip is approximately **10.64–11.48 V** (nominal 11.03 V): above a 9 V +5% source (9.45 V), below BQ's 14 V recommended maximum. Tie UVLO to IN to disable the IC's otherwise enabled factory ~15 V UVLO; leave SHDN unconnected to use its internal approximately 2.7 V pull-up for autonomous operation (SHDN recommended pin range is 0–4 V, so it must not be tied to 21 V IN); tie MODE to RTN for automatic retry; leave dVdT open initially, subject to inrush review. Use **5.36 kΩ** ILIM-to-RTN for 2.23 A typical/2.35 A maximum threshold, above the current BQ ILIM ceiling. C180 1 µF / 50 V is now a local eFuse input capacitor; the protected charger C100 is the output capacitor. Their exact effective capacitance and input inrush remain to be checked. Connect thermal pad to RTN copper per datasheet. RTN and GND are tied to the common board ground; TI says this disables its reverse-input-polarity protection, so no reverse-input-fault survival is claimed. The internal back-to-back FET reverse-output blocking is a separate function. The output must be checked during an already-on 9→15/20 V step: TI specifies about 6 µs OVP disable delay. BQ25895 SW has a 16 V absolute maximum, so its 22 V VBUS non-switching absolute rating does not make an unprotected live transition safe. The captured design therefore adds a 10 V standoff TVS after the eFuse, on the protected VBUS_PD rail. Taiwan Semiconductor SMA6J10A M2G (LCSC C2444429) has 15.7 V maximum clamp at 38.2 A rated 10/1000 µs peak at 25 °C; the eFuse current limit and local output capacitance reduce the 6 µs excursion. Fast-trip current and layout parasitics must be checked on hardware; the 2.35 A steady overload maximum is not an instantaneous surge ceiling. [Taiwan Semiconductor SMA6J datasheet](https://services.taiwansemi.com/storage/resources/datasheet/SMA6J%20SERIES_E2102.pdf); [BQ25895 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25895.pdf), Table 7-1; [TPS2660 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf), §§7.3–7.6, 9.3.

**D6 SMBJ10A cannot remain on raw `USB_VBUS`.** Its 10 V working standoff and 11.1–12.3 V breakdown mean a sustained 15/20 V contract would continuously avalanche it before the eFuse. Replace raw D6 with **TI TVS2200DRVR** (WSON-6 2×2 mm; LCSC **C523793**), with 22 V working standoff and 24.6–27.6 V breakdown. It is intended for 20 V USB-C VBUS and avoids a sustained shunt at 21 V. Its specified maximum 8/20 µs clamp is **28.4 V at 40 A**, however, versus STUSB4500's **28 V absolute maximum on VDD and high-voltage pins**. Trace inductance adds further overshoot. Thus this selection is a sound DC protection direction and strong surge candidate, but **does not prove a 40 A surge safe for STUSB4500**. State the board's actual surge/ESD target and validate the connector-to-TVS-to-controller layout and waveform; if 40 A with a strict 28 V pin ceiling is required, add a genuinely lower guaranteed clamp or a suitable series surge-limiting/protection stage. A generic SMBJ22A is worse for coordination: its rated clamp is around 35.5 V. The captured downstream Taiwan Semiconductor SMA6J10A M2G is located **only on the protected side**; the old raw SMBJ10A is removed. [TI TVS2200 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tvs2200.pdf), §§7.6, 9.2; [STUSB4500 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stusb4500.pdf), Tables 20–21; [Littelfuse SMBJ datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/tvs-diode-smbj-datasheet?assetguid=5f9a36be-8f4d-4afd-8fab-b1027249607b).

STUSB4500's VDD and VBUS high-voltage pins are specified for **0–22 V operation / 28 V absolute**, so 21 V DC is within the former. Its CC short-to-VBUS absolute rating is 22 V; the existing CC device and routing need separate ESD/pin stress validation. The existing STL9P3LLH6 PMOS pair is **30 V VDS / ±20 V VGS**. Present R10 100 kΩ source-to-gate and R11 22 kΩ gate-to-ST sink limit steady VGS at 21 V to about −17.2 V even without a clamp, but a 28 V raw transient would pull it near −23 V. Add a **12 V gate-source Zener**, cathode to the shared source, anode to the shared gate, e.g. Diodes Incorporated **BZT52C12-7-F** SOD-123 (LCSC **C124196**). At 21 V, approximate gate sink current is 9 V/22 kΩ = 0.41 mA and Zener current about 0.29 mA; at a 28.4 V clamp they remain below 1 mA, compared with ST's 3 mA low-level output test condition. Verify the Zener voltage at this low current and transient gate waveform during layout qualification. Q1/Q2's 30 V VDS has little headroom to the TVS2200 28.4 V rated clamp. [ST PMOS datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stl9p3llh6.pdf), Tables 1/3; [STUSB4500 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stusb4500.pdf), Table 22.

Upstream C2/C4/C5/C6 are shown as 25 V. Their nominal voltage rating exceeds a 21 V DC high contract, but exact MPN, bias derating, surge margin, and placement are open. R4 1 kΩ, currently a 0603 in the ST VBUS sense/discharge path, can see up to roughly 21²/1000 = 0.44 W while the internal discharge is active if its full supply appears across it. Increase R4 to approximately **3.3 kΩ in a qualified 1206 pulse-rated resistor** (about 0.134 W at 21 V; about 0.244 W at 28.4 V transient), and verify that ST's VBUS sensing/discharge timing remains compliant. The 470 Ω R1 bypass/diode network and R2 downstream discharge network also need pulse-power review for the final rail position. [STUSB4500 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stusb4500.pdf), §§2.2.12, 7.3.

Safe startup policy remains **program and read back STUSB4500 NVM to two PDOs, 5 V and 9 V, with `POWER_ONLY_ABOVE_5V=0`**. Its factory 15 V/20 V requests must not be counted as intentional operating modes. The hardware cutoff makes a wrongly programmed or reset unit survive those contracts by denying power to the charger; on battery, operation continues from BAT/SYS as designed, and with no battery the system loses external power until VBUS returns below the OVP release threshold. Firmware should not repeatedly request an unsupported high PDO. Hardware verification must cover cold boot with factory NVM, 5 V/9 V operation, 9→15/20 V renegotiation, return to 5 V, cable unplug, and a depleted/absent pack.

## Alternative: actually operate from 15/20 V

TI **BQ25792** or **BQ25798** are 1–4 cell NVDC buck-boost charger families with 3.6–24 V operating input and 30 V absolute input, so a 20 V +5% contract is within their intended input range. This is a substantial charger-sheet redesign: package/pinout, switch network/inductor, configuration registers, default behavior, TS/ship/wake controls, SYS levels, capacitor ratings, source policy and thermal design all need rework and validation. Their 20 V operation still requires the raw TVS and Q1/Q2 gate/surge coordination above. Select this path only if using the extra 15/20 V power becomes a product requirement. [TI BQ25792 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25792.pdf); [TI BQ25798 product page](https://www.ti.com/product/BQ25798).
