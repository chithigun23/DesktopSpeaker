### 3.2 Problems
1. **SW100 still cannot be pressed through the lid.**
   - The redrawn TE footprint (switch moved 2.295 mm, y 25.245 -> 22.95) puts the actuator tip exactly at the board edge (Y 202.10). That is 0.50 mm short of the lid inner face and 4.50 mm behind the outer face (RT-5 estimate 4.5 mm: confirmed). The switch body is 1.35 mm from the lid.
   - A printed plunger in the 5.0 bore must bridge 4.50 mm plus its protrusion and travel.
   - Only 0.5 mm is free behind the lid, so a retaining collar needs a counterbore in the lid inner face. This is not modelled: v14 keeps the v13 lid.
   - Switch travel was not extracted (the TE PDF is a drawing without text).
2. **Woofer screw engagement is marginal** (unchanged): M3x20 gives 3.4 / 4.4 mm in the 5.7 mm insert; M3x22 would give 5.4-6.4 mm.
3. The pcb.glb vs CAD component box differs for SW102 (0.77) and C275 (1.0). This is an exporter difference, as in v13.

### 3.3 Screw table
Unchanged from v13 (ND65 M3x10 ok, woofer M3x20 marginal, PCB M3x16 ok, lid M3x10 ok, feet studs ok).
