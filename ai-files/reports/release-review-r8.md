## FAIL 8a — U11 (TPS25730D) pads 20, 23, 32, 34: mask and paste openings are 0.1 mm combs

- Where: footprint `DesktopSpeaker:TPS25730D` (library `DesktopSpeaker-kicad/kicad-library/footprint/TPS25730D.kicad_mod` lines 60-111 and 204-255, and the board-embedded copy of U11 at (137.4, 55.8), rotation 0).
- What: the four merged power pads are `(layers "F.Cu")` only — pad 20 `/Battery_Charger/VBUS_PD` (pins 20-22, 0.65 x 1.2 at 140.325, 56.462), pad 23 `/USB_VBUS` (pins 23-25, at 140.325, 55.138), pad 32 `/USB_VBUS` (pins 32-33, 0.6 x 0.65 at 137.2, 53.875), pad 34 `GND` (pins 34-35, at 136.4, 53.875). Their mask and paste openings are hand-drawn `fp_poly` items on F.Mask and F.Paste shaped as combs: pads 20/23 get a 0.1 x 1.0 mm spine plus three 0.35 x 0.1 mm fingers (bbox 0.45 x 1.0); pads 32/34 get a 0.5 x 0.1 mm bar plus two 0.1 x 0.35 mm legs (bbox 0.5 x 0.45). Every other pin of U11 has a normal 0.2 x 0.55 mm opening.
- Gerber evidence: `DesktopSpeaker-F_Mask.gts` and `DesktopSpeaker-F_Paste.gtp` each contain exactly 4 G36/G37 regions, at bbox x 140.100..140.550 / y 54.638..55.638 and 55.962..56.962, and x 136.150..136.650 and 136.950..137.450 / y 53.650..54.100; signed distance from the pad-20/23 centres to the nearest opening is -0.05 mm (inside a 0.1 mm slit), from the pad-32/34 centres +0.125 mm (outside any opening). Rendered crop (F.Cu/F.Mask/F.Paste): `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-chithi-Desktop-DesktopSpeaker/48064651-bbac-4d10-bf68-aa2bb3ba73e8/scratchpad/review-r8/u11-crop.png` (session scratch; the "E" shapes right of the EP and the "U" shapes above it).
- Why it makes the boards unusable: 0.1 mm stencil slits in a 0.12 mm stencil have an area ratio of about 0.3-0.4 (release needs >= 0.66), so the IN (USB_VBUS), OUT (VBUS_PD) and GND 34/35 pins of the only USB power switch receive practically no paste, and JLC's LPI mask cannot reliably resolve 0.1 mm slits either. The USB power path (and so charging and USB operation) would be open or marginal on delivered boards. KiCad DRC cannot flag this (no check for SMD pads without mask/paste layers), which is why all gates passed.
- Minimal fix (footprint only, no routing change): replace each of the 8 comb polygons by a solid rectangle equal to its bounding box (F.Mask and F.Paste: 0.45 x 1.0 at local (2.7..3.15, 0.1625..1.1625) and (2.7..3.15, -1.1625..-0.1625); 0.5 x 0.45 at local (-1.25..-0.75, -2.15..-1.7) and (-0.45..0.05, -2.15..-1.7)), in the library file and in the board's U11 (or update U11 from the library). The bboxes already keep 0.325 mm mask web between pads 20 and 23 and 0.3 mm between 32 and 34, so the IN/OUT openings stay separate (giving the pads themselves F.Mask/F.Paste would leave only 0.124 mm between the 20/23 openings and JLC would merge them, bridging IN to OUT — do not do that without shrinking the pads). Then: DRC (expect 0/0 unchanged), `fab_export.sh` again, re-check the 4 regions, re-zip, update ORDER-NOTES/handover.

## CONCERN 8c — U6/U7 exposed pads: 100 % paste over 16 open thermal vias each

U6 and U7 pad 33 (3.45 x 3.45 mm) carry full-pad paste apertures with 16 x 0.6/0.3 vias inside the opening (tenting does not apply inside a pad opening). Paste will partly drain into the vias during reflow; usual practice is a windowed stencil (50-70 %) as already done for U11/Q103/U19. Assembly-quality only; JLC builds such boards. Optional fix: windowed paste pads in the TAS5825MRHBR footprint, or accept.
