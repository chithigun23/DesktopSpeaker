# Storage switch sourcing — 2026-10-05

Sourcing only; no schematic, symbol, library, BOM or plan changes. Existing instances remain `SW100 / WAKE_QON` and `SW101 / BAT_DISCONNECT`. The active Battery_Charger sheet connects SW100 from BQ25895 QON to GND and SW101 in series between protected pack J5.1 and `BAT_PACK`; J5.2 NTC and J5.3 pack ground remain unchanged. SW101 therefore disconnects the board's protected-pack positive feed in addition to the charger BATFET/electronic-off mode.

## SW100 — QON momentary

**Recommended candidate:** TE Connectivity `1977066-1`, LCSC `C2972219`, 50 mA / 12 V DC tactile momentary SPST-NO. LCSC page source (stock captured by direct page read): [C2972219 product page](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Tactile-Switches_TE-Connectivity_C2972219.html), **2,009 shown in stock** on 2026-10-05. The LCSC page description and TE drawing identify a 50 mA, 12 VDC contact rating; this is a signal switch, not a load switch. The 12 V rating is above the QON signal domain. The BQ25895 datasheet specifies QON's internal pull-up as 200 kΩ and the ship-mode exit pulse as 1.25 s min / 2.25 s max for its stated temperature range; the existing >=2.3 s user instruction is consistent with that maximum.

The TE drawing [downloaded to `../datasheets/TE_1977066-1_datasheet.pdf`](../datasheets/TE_1977066-1_datasheet.pdf) gives a right-angle SMT part with 4.50 mm body width, approximately 3.3 mm body height, 2.00 mm actuator, two 0.90 mm locating holes, and three contact lands. Its suggested copper land pattern spans 5.00 × 3.20 mm, with a keepout beneath the body. It has no LCSC EDA footprint/model shown in the product snapshot; use TE's drawing land pattern if chosen and verify against the current symbol footprint map. A manufacturer STEP download is linked on the TE product page, but was not downloaded in this sourcing-only brief. The part is small and board-mounted, so an enclosure button/plunger must be designed to reach the side actuator without side-loading the PCB.

## SW101 — protected-pack positive disconnect

**Do not select a switch yet.** The target is a protected 1S lithium-ion pack around 10 Ah, but neither the pack's continuous/pulse current capability nor system's worst-case battery current has been fixed. Capacity alone does not establish either. The later audio power stage and speaker load are unresolved, and existing power reviews explicitly leave SW101's DC continuous/pulse rating open. A switch's AC ampere rating cannot be substituted for a DC rating: DC arcs do not receive AC zero-crossings, so use an explicit manufacturer DC contact rating at or above the pack's maximum voltage and calculated current. Also require suitable make/break/inrush, fault-clearing coordination, cycle life and connector/wire ratings.

**Catalog reference only, not a selection:** Switch Components `SRC1-1A-DC-1-B`, LCSC `C7369595`, is a maintained panel rocker, 2P SPST OFF-ON, with manufacturer-authored datasheet rating **20 A at 14 VDC / 10 A at 24 VDC**. The LCSC product metadata currently says Out of Stock (`stockNumber: 0`) and its generic voltage attribute is mislabeled under AC; do not rely on that attribute. The downloaded manufacturer drawing [at `../datasheets/Switch_Components_SRC1-1A-DC-1-B_C7369595.pdf`](../datasheets/Switch_Components_SRC1-1A-DC-1-B_C7369595.pdf) is the rating evidence. It specifies a 36.8 × 15.3 mm panel cutout, 0.75–5.0 mm panel thickness and two 0.187 in (4.75 mm) quick-connect blades. It reports up to 50 mΩ contact resistance; at that maximum, I²R would be 5 W at 10 A or 20 W at 20 A, so the top-line rating alone does not prove acceptable contact temperature/drop in this enclosure. It is out of stock, and the datasheet supplies no pulse/inrush qualification for this battery application.

Another LCSC catalog reference, Switch Components `TA2-1F-DC-6-O` / `C6546190`, has a clear 21 A at 14 VDC rating on its datasheet, but is also out of stock and is 3P SPDT ON-OFF-ON, not the required simple maintained SPST. The manufacturer drawing [downloaded at `../datasheets/Switch_Components_TA2-1F-DC-6-O_C6546190.pdf`](../datasheets/Switch_Components_TA2-1F-DC-6-O_C6546190.pdf) is included to document why AC-only ratings were rejected; this is not a recommended workaround.

**Provisional architecture:** retain SW101 as an off-board mechanical break in the protected pack-positive lead. Bring J5's pack positive and board `BAT_PACK` to a properly rated, serviceable connector/terminal arrangement, then use a panel-mounted, maintained DC switch with harness conductors selected after current is known. Do not force a high-current switch onto a PCB footprint or assume PCB traces/connector contacts are adequate. Select SW101 only after allocating worst-case battery continuous and pulse current and determining whether users may switch it under load. If a board-integrated panel-switch harness is desired, include a two-pin power connector footprint with current-rated copper/terminals rather than a low-current generic PCB switch footprint. Keep the existing series relationship and pack protection arrangement.
