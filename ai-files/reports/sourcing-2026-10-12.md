## 3. Battery disconnect rocker SW101

LCSC finds: no suitable in-stock round 20 mm rocker with a stated DC rating.
- Shou Han KCD1-201-R C5884429: not available; PC-pin, 23 x 20.5 mm, height 25 mm (https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Rocker-Switches_SHOU-HAN-KCD1-201-R_C5884429.html).
- Switch Components RC-1A-DC-1-UL C6637735: out of stock, $7.45, no DC rating or mechanicals listed.
- Shou Han KCD1-202-R C5884426 (20 A, solder lug): stock not confirmed, datasheet not readable.

Fallback candidates (Digi-Key):
1. CW Industries GRB112B802BR1 (red LED, SPST): 7,073 in stock, $5.09 qty 1 / $4.39 qty 10, 12 week factory lead time (https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/cw-industries/GRB112B802BR1/2349683). Drawing (https://www.cwind.com/sites/cwind/files/grb112b802br1.pdf, read as image): 20 A 14 VDC (16 A 125 VAC, 10 A 250 VAC), hole 20.2 mm with a 2.2 mm key notch, 0.187 in (4.8 mm) faston terminals x3, flange 25 mm, 11.5 mm above panel, overall 22.4 mm (terminals about 11 mm behind the panel, approximate), IP65. Panel thickness 1.5-2.5 mm (the drawing also shows 19.0 mm hole, 0.7-2.5 mm). Does NOT cover 4 mm. The LED needs 12 VDC (can be left unused only if pin wiring allows; lamp is dependent on pin 3).
2. Switch Components RA1-1A-DC-2-GL (green LED 14 VDC): $2.79 marketplace, 20 A DC, 20.2 mm cutout, 0.187 in faston (https://azcus.digikey.com/en/products/detail/switch-components/RA1-1A-DC-2-GL/11492856); thickness and depth not checked.
3. E-Switch RR511D1121 (20.0 mm cutout, 20 A AC only, no DC rating) - not recommended.

Recommendation: GRB112B802BR1, with a local 2.0-2.5 mm pocket/counterbore in the 4 mm lid around the hole (the snap-in needs 1.5-2.5 mm). 14 VDC rating is below the 16 V maximum: acceptable for a battery-side disconnect at 3.6-4.2 V per cell only if this switch is on the 1S battery rail; if on a 12-16 V rail, verify with the maker.

2. CM Batteries 1S2P 21700 10 Ah (https://cmbatteries.com/project/1s2p-battery-pack/): 10 Ah nominal (9.7 min), 3.65 V, 10 A continuous, 25-40 A burst, charge 2 A std / 5 A max, 152.8 g, samples with no MOQ. Outline, NTC, connector, price, lead time not listed: quote.
3. DIY: 2 x Samsung INR21700-50S + 1S PCM (about 10 A) + cell holder/nickel strip. A PCM with a 10 k NTC lead was not found in retail searches (1S boards found are 1-10 A without NTC, e.g. https://scccltd.com/products/18650-battery-protection-module-3-7v-10a). Not verified.
Also seen: Epoch 21700 5000 mAh single-cell pack with PCB, 5 A only (https://www.18650batterystore.com:443/products/epoch-batteries-21700-5000mah-5a-battery-with-pcb-and-6-leads): too weak and 1P.

Recommendation: ask Keeppower and CM Batteries to quote the 1S2P with a 10 k NTC (B 3435 K), PCM >= 10 A continuous and 5 A charge, specifying the NTC part so it matches the charger 103AT network. Australia shipping not confirmed for any (cell shipping needs UN38.3 documents, which both list).
