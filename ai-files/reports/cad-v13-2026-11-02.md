## 2. Changes (parameters in `P`, `ai-files/cad/v13/scripts/build_speaker_cad.py`)

### 2.1 Tang Band W3-2052SC (model `TangBand_W3-2052SC.step`)
- Removed: round 86 ring pad (`ring_t` 5.5 -> 0), 83.4 x 1.0 flange recess (`w_recess` 1.0 -> 0), the round 82.55 x 4 stand-in and the 77 mm bolt circle.
- New: square 82.55 frame (R4) with the factory 3.3 mm gasket seated directly on the floor inner face (z 3.0, `w_seat` 0). Holes on the 96.52 circle = 68.25 square (`w_bc` 96.52, `w_hole_sq` 68.25, `w_hole` 3.4), frame block 13.3, total 47.75.
- Aperture 76.2 kept. Sealing land on the flats: 3.18 mm. Pilot edge to aperture 8.16 mm (v12: pilots broke 1.6 mm into it). Hole edge to frame edge 5.45 mm.
- Screws: M3x20, head on the frame back (z 19.60). Ruthex M3x5.7 inserts with pilot 4.0 x 6.7. The pilot needs 7.7 mm of material under the seat, so it sits in four outside bosses (Ø8, teardrop with a 45 deg point toward the baffle for support-free printing). The bosses reach 4.7 mm below the floor and leave a 1.0 mm skin, so the chamber stays sealed.
- Why not a raised pad: the magnet top is at z 50.75, only 3.25 mm under the roof (z 54). Raising the seat to give the screw more thread would push the magnet into the roof.
- Woofer grille Ø90 -> Ø86 (`w_grille_d`), 1.26 mm from the bosses.
- Clearances: frame to partition 14.94 mm, to lid 11.94 mm, to side walls 16.73 mm. Magnet cup to the H2 PCB-screw boss 2.88 mm.
- Woofer centre offset changed from mid-chamber -2.0 to +1.5 mm (`w_cy_off`; Y 151.50 -> 149.38). At the old offset the H2 boss touched the magnet cup (2.2 mm3).
- Chamber volume: the real woofer displaces 0.103 L; the stand-in displaced 0.045 L. v13 also excludes the 0.020 L of air in front of the cone, which is open to the outside through the aperture (`woofer_front_air.brep`, used in `check_cad.py`).
  - `CH_DISPLACED_L` 0.056 -> 0.1224 L (env `CH_DISP_L`).
  - Chamber length 98.2 -> 109.4 mm; partition Y 101.39 -> 90.17; net 0.525 L.
  - v12 counted the air inside its hollow stand-in cone as chamber volume, so its 0.525 L was really about 0.485 L (estimate).
- Pigtail: the harness tube now ends at the frame rib (+X).

### 3.2 Problems
1. **SW100 cannot be pressed through the lid.**
   - With the TE model on the draft footprint, the actuator tip is 2.30 mm inside the board edge: 2.80 mm short of the lid inner face and 6.80 mm behind the outer face.
   - The 5 mm bore only admits a pin.
   - Fix it with the footprint correction (actuator at the board edge) and/or a printed lid plunger.
2. **Woofer screw engagement is marginal.** M3x20 gives 3.4 mm of thread with the gasket free and 4.4 mm at 1 mm compression, in a 5.7 mm insert. M3x22 would give 5.4-6.4 mm and still fit the 6.7 mm pilot.
3. **Footprint errors, modelled as drawn and not fixed here:**
   - J2/J3: front NPTH pegs 1.0 mm off.
   - U1: pad 56 off by 3.37 mm.
   - SW100: draft footprint (pads, pegs, bracket pads).
   - C275: D8 can on an 8x10 land.
