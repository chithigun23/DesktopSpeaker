# Current power circuitry critical review — 2026-10-05

Read-only review of active power sheets and local manufacturer datasheets. No schematic/library/BOM/plan edits and no ERC. Refreshed the pin-net checks against [current-review-netlist.xml](current-review-netlist.xml), exported from the active root project using KiCad 10.0.6 on 2026-10-05, and cross-checked the latest connectivity review reports. Electrical measurements, PCB routing, thermal behavior and transient response are outside this evidence.

## Findings

1. **High — source permission is not enforced in hardware or yet by firmware. Demonstrated integration gap; no incorrect pin connection found.** The charger is deliberately charge-disabled by R103/Q100 until MCU control, but the MCU is not connected. BQ25895 reset IINLIM is 500 mA; its datasheet REG00 reset behavior does not mean a USB host has granted 500 mA (local `BQ25895.pdf`, §8.4.1). PCM2902C legacy USB descriptor requests 100 mA (`PCM2902C.pdf`, USB descriptor tables; recorded in `power-passive-inrush-review.md`). The 220 Ω ILIM path is a hardware ceiling around 1.45–1.77 A, not source detection. A connected USB host could therefore face system draw above its allowed current even while charging is disabled. **Recommended:** before codec/MCU integration, add a policy that holds both downstream rails off and limits input current to the source's initial permission, then raises it only from verified Type-C/PD or explicit known-adapter selection. Ensure defaults after reset/watchdog remain safe. Do not present the BQ 500 mA reset as source authorization.

2. **High — nominal 5 V codec rail lacks a guaranteed upper bound below PCM2902C's operating maximum. Qualification gap, not a demonstrated overvoltage.** U14 TPS61023 divider sets 4.950 V nominal. The TPS61023 datasheet does not list a maximum PFM feedback reference, while selected 1% resistors can add roughly 1.5% ratio drift across an illustrative 75 °C span before reference and ripple (`TPS61023.pdf`; `power-passive-inrush-review.md`). PCM2902C maximum operating supply is 5.25 V (`PCM2902C.pdf`, Recommended Operating Conditions). The expected rail is nominally in range, but the current evidence does not bound PFM/ripple/temperature transients below 5.25 V. **Recommended:** before connecting U14 to the codec, choose a lower feedback target or a regulator with a guaranteed max, then verify load-step/startup output over the intended load and temperature range.

3. **Medium — 5 V source headroom can collapse at substantial input current; startup ramp estimate does not close the power budget. Qualification gap.** TPS26600 max RON allowance plus paired PMOS allowance is about 295 mΩ under their specified conditions. In the existing illustrative 4.75 V source/0.20 Ω cable case, 1.5 A leaves about 4.008 V at BQ VBUS; eFuse input is only about 4.383 V against its 4.2 V minimum. At 1.77 A, the eFuse allowance dissipates about 0.78 W. C181 now provides a controlled ramp (nominal 5.255 V/ms; estimated 3.41–8.33 V/ms before aging/bias) and reduces the modeled capacitor inrush, but excludes dynamic SYS/rail loads and actual source/cable response (`TPS2660.pdf` §9.3.4; `5v-input-headroom.md`; `power-passive-inrush-review.md`). **Recommended:** limit source current from measured VBUS/VINDPM/IDPM, reduce charge current first, and qualify cold/low-source/hot-board startup. Do not claim full 5 V / 1.5 A operation or battery-absent full-load operation from these calculations.

4. **Medium — eFuse OVP improves sustained 15/20 V fault handling; transient coordination remains unproven. Known qualification gap, not evidence that the old unprotected path remains.** Current U18 TPS26600 configuration has UVLO tied to IN, SHDN left open for its internal pull-up, OVP divider nominally about 11.03 V (reported corner 10.64–11.48 V), and C181 on dVdT. This supersedes the 2026-10-04 report's old finding that PD could pass unblocked to the charger. However, the datasheet's roughly 6 µs OVP response, raw TVS2200 clamp maximum 28.4 V, and downstream SMA6J10A clamp 15.7 V at its stated surge current do not prove survival for a fast 9→20 V transition or rated surge; 15.7 V is close to BQ SW's 16 V absolute maximum (`TPS2660.pdf`; `TVS2200.pdf`; `TaiwanSemi_SMA6J.pdf`; `BQ25895.pdf` absolute maximum table). **Recommended:** validate the worst PD transition and port surge with layout parasitics, or add a lower-clamp/appropriately coordinated protection stage if required by the target environment. Keep the 5/9 V NVM policy and readback requirement as defense in depth.

5. **Medium — gauge isolation leakage and BAT-collapse behavior can dominate the electronic-off budget. Datasheet limit / qualification issue, not a proven fault.** U13/U16/U17 are TS5A3167DBVR, VCC=BAT_PACK, IN low, COM tied to AO-side bus and NC to gauge-side; TI explicitly says IN low connects COM to NC. The three devices isolate when their BAT supply is absent, but remain on while the pack is present. TI powered-off analog-port leakage is up to ±25 µA per port under the 0–3.6 V conditions (higher at 5.5 V); the nominal 0.03 µA supply-current figure is not a bound on system leakage through attached nets (`TS5A3167.pdf`, pin truth table and electrical characteristics). Their guaranteed operating VCC minimum is 1.65 V, so behavior during a collapsing protected pack between valid operation and VCC=0 is not established. MAX17048 pin absolute maximum is not the same as functional partial-bias behavior. **Recommended:** measure AO/gauge lines and battery current through shutdown/pack-collapse transitions with real pull-ups and MCU pins while keeping the operating modes separate: the ~35–37 µA subtotal is preliminary normal inactive operation with BATFET enabled and charger monitor off; it is not ship-mode current. In ship mode the BQ25895 datasheet specifies 12 µA typical / 23 µA max BAT discharge with BATFET disabled and monitor off (TJ<85 °C), plus the still-powered gauge and other unresolved leakage. Do not reuse 32 µA BATFET-enabled current for ship mode. Consider pin-level isolation or changed sequencing if the measured drain is too high.

6. **Medium — pack thermistor curve is unresolved, so charging-temperature window is provisional.** BQ TS uses the 103AT-2 curve assumption with the captured resistor divider; the selected pack only specifies a nominal 10 kΩ sensor so far. The BQ datasheet recommends 103AT-2 and suspends charging when TS is out of its programmed window (local `BQ25895.pdf`, pin description and §8.3 TS monitoring). A different 10 kΩ curve can shift real trip temperatures. This is a protective false-trip or missed-temperature-limit risk, but no specific error can be calculated until the pack sensor curve is known. **Recommended:** match the divider to the purchased pack NTC beta/table and verify hot/cold trip points before enabling charge. Keep charge disabled until that selection is fixed.

7. **Low — ship wake and physical-disconnect implementation are coherent on paper; switch ratings and sequencing are unqualified.** SW100 shorts QON to ground and is annotated for a ≥2.3 s hold; charger datasheet gives ship exit via QON low for its ship-mode deglitch duration, USB insertion also wakes, while a long QON hold with no VBUS can reset SYS. SW101 opens protected pack positive upstream of BAT_PACK board loads. Pin/net relationships are supported by the latest storage-protection review. Neither switch has a selected MPN/current rating, and MCU-controlled shutdown sequencing is unfinished. **Recommended:** select SW101 for pack peak/continuous current and expected cycle life; validate that shutdown acknowledgment occurs before users open it and that QON long-hold behavior is acceptable.

8. **Low — remaining regulator and capacitor limits are open design qualification, not demonstrated miswiring.** The captured U14/U15 topologies and feedback values match their intended rails; U15 pin mapping, inductor, and output banks were reviewed. TPS63802 22 µF outputs and charger PMID/REGN banks were increased/selected, but effective capacitance figures use typical bias curves and illustrative derating rather than guaranteed minima; L/C transient and stability behavior with eventual module/audio loads is untested (`TPS63802.pdf` §§8.3/10.2; `BQ25895.pdf` §8.2.3; `pd-charger-capacitors.json`). U15's 500 mA output capability reserve is not a current clamp. **Recommended:** close effective-capacitance, inductor saturation and load-step checks once actual loads are allocated, then verify on PCB.

## Checks that passed at schematic level

- The updated U18 configuration and local input capacitor are recorded as corrected; the latest handover reports 13 integrated PD pin relations and preserved retained physical pin groups.
- BQ /CE is default-disabled through R103/Q100; SW100 is QON-only and SW101 is the positive pack disconnect. TS divider is the known provisional 103AT-2 example, not represented as a matched pack selection.
- U14 feedback gives about 4.95 V and U15 feedback gives about 3.819 V nominal. U15 pin map and L3 routing are documented as matching the datasheet; no mismatched pin connection is evidenced by the current reports.
- Gauge-switch pins and changed nets are documented in the 2026-10-04 handover. Their isolation promise is only for BAT absent / powered-off mode, not for electronic ship mode with a live pack.

The fresh netlist confirms U18 IN1/2 on raw PMOS output with OVP divider and U18 OUT15/16 on `VBUS_PD` shared with BQ VBUS; U18 dVdT12 connects to C181 and its ILIM11 to R182. Q100.3 shares BQ /CE pin9 and R103, while Q100 gate is `CHG_ENABLE` and source is GND. SW100 is QON12-to-GND; SW101 separates J5.1 from BAT_PACK. U4 SYS pins15/16 feed `SYS_RAW`; U12 and U14/U15 inputs share it. TS5A3167 COM2 goes to AO-side buses, NC1 to MAX17048 pins, VCC5 to BAT_PACK and active-low IN4 to GND, consistent with normally connecting the gauge while BAT is present and isolation when BAT is absent. U14/U15 output and feedback relationships match intended rails.

I found no demonstrated wrong power pin/net in the fresh netlist. Findings 1–8 are enforceable-source policy or component/transition qualification gaps, except that absent source-aware limiting could cause a real overdraw if the unfinished integration is used as-is. MCU/audio unfinished sections are treated as planned work, not defects. The separate main-agent ERC reports 358 findings: root 326 are attributed to unfinished sections; USB_PD has one missing-source annotation; Battery_Charger has 21 unspecified-pin-type warnings; Fuel_Gauge_Power has 8 unspecified-pin-type warnings and 2 missing-source annotations; Bluetooth_Power and Logic_Audio_Power have none. These categories are not evidence of a demonstrated power-net defect. KiCad CLI also rejected the standalone TS5A3167 and TPS63802 library files due to unsupported version headers; diagnostic copies with only the header changed loaded, and the active schematic-embedded symbols/netlist were inspected. No project library edits were made.
