# Power fix follow-up — 2026-10-05

Read-only check of `power-fixes-after-cleanup.xml` against the active USB_PD and Fuel_Gauge_Power captures and local manufacturer datasheets. No active project edits or ERC were made.

**No immediate physical-pin wiring error found.** U18 TPS26600: IN1/2 and UVLO3 receive the PMOS output; SHDN7 is open for its internal 2–3.4 V pull-up, rather than tied to high-voltage IN; OVP5 has R180/R181 100 kΩ/12.1 kΩ divider; ILIM11 has 5.36 kΩ to RTN; RTN8, GND9, MODE6 and EP17 ground; OUT15/16 feed protected `VBUS_PD`. C180 1 µF/50 V is on IN/GND. D6 TVS2200 IN4–6 is on raw VBUS and GND1–3/EP7 grounded. D7 cathode is on shared PMOS source, anode on gates. D8 Taiwan Semiconductor SMA6J10A cathode1 is on protected VBUS_PD, anode2 grounded. TI TPS2660 explicitly says UVLO-to-IN is correct if the external UVLO function is unused, and its SHDN internal pull-up supports open-pin enable (§§9.3.1, 9.3.5.7).

Gauge U5 SDA8/SCL7/ALRT5 now each directly wire to TS5A3167 U13/U16/U17 NC1 respectively; COM2 routes to board bus/alert, IN4 and GND3 ground, VCC5 is protected BAT_PACK. C130–C132 each decouple BAT_PACK to ground. This matches the TI TS5A3167 DBV pin table and active-low truth table. Its powered-off isolation is specified, with ±25 µA full-temperature port leakage at 0–3.6 V signal voltage; MAX17048 digital absolute limits need no VDD+0.3 assumption. Battery-absent USB operation still needs hardware qualification.

**Remaining qualification, not proved defects:** TPS26600 OVP cut-off has finite delay (about 6 µs typical); D8's 15.7 V rated 25°C clamp at 38.2 A leaves small margin to the BQ25895 16 V SW absolute maximum, and rail overshoot depends on actual waveform/temperature/layout. Raw TVS2200's 28.4 V rated clamp similarly does not prove compliance with STUSB4500's 28 V absolute limit at the controller pin. RTN and GND are tied; per TI §9.3.5.5 this disables reverse-input-polarity protection, a deliberate USB-C topology tradeoff, not a reverse-fault guarantee. Verify these with target surge waveforms and board layout. No PCB or physical tests exist yet.
